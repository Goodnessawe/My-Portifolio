IrenlaAn AI trading terminal for retail traders, built to an institutional standard

The name is Yoruba, Ire Ńlá, great goodness. It reads live markets, explains its signals in plain English, and places trades through real brokers under hard risk limits. Press the kill switch above.

  • Next.js 14 and TypeScript over a Python FastAPI back end, SQLite today with a Postgres compatibility layer ready for cut-over.
  • Live market data over WebSockets from Deriv and Binance, plus Yahoo Finance and a Finnhub economic calendar.
  • AI Strategist on the Claude API with tool calling: account summary, open positions and instrument analysis. Tier limits are enforced in the architecture, so locked fields never leave the server.
  • Broker layer: TradeLocker and cTrader live and placing real trades, a Deriv Multipliers driver, risk-based lot sizing, trailing stop, break-even and an emergency kill switch.
  • Signal engine with Bayesian-shrinkage confidence and its own test benches to prove an edge before real money is switched on.
  • Admin console on a separate codebase: super admin and admin roles, broadcasts, Resend incident email, support desk. Stripe billing built server-side.

    C-traseA school operating system: records, attendance, fees, buses and marking in one place

    Built from conversations with teachers, school administrators and company leadership. Mastery plus trace: every child's learning, kept and followed. Switch roles above and watch what each one can touch.

    • Node.js and Fastify with SQLite. Closed sign-in: phone and one-time code for adults, code and PIN for students. Nobody joins without the school adding them.
    • Seven roles, principal to state authority, checked at the route and the service layer, so a missed check in one place is still caught in the other.
    • Attendance with instant parent SMS, tests and report cards, and a bursar ledger where parents upload payment proof and get a real receipt.
    • Automatic bus tracking: GPS ticks off stops by path-crossing maths, Traccar over the OsmAnd protocol, Leaflet maps with a Google Maps switch.
    • Snap-scripts marking: a teacher photographs a script, Claude vision suggests marks, the teacher always confirms.
    • Proof it holds: 210+ automated tests, Playwright visual and accessibility checks, and a mutation script that breaks one security rule at a time to prove the tests notice.

    Claim LensInsurance fraud detection with k-means clustering

    Honest claims look like each other. Fraud usually does not. Cluster the claims, measure how far each sits from its group, and send the strangers to a person for review.

    • Unsupervised, so it works without a labelled history of past fraud, which most insurers do not have.
    • Distance from centroid becomes a review score, not an accusation. A person makes the final call.
    • Python with the standard scientific stack for cleaning, scaling and clustering.

    Chapter VI · Method

    How I work

    1

    Prove it, then ship it

    C-trase has a script that breaks its own security rules one at a time. If a test does not fail, the gap is closed before a school sees it.

    2

    Numbers must not flatter

    Irenla's win rate leaves out expired signals, and real-money auto-trading stays off until enough measured trades say the edge is real.

    3

    Security by shape

    Tier-locked data is never sent and then hidden; it is never built into the response. Admin lives in its own codebase, away from the client app.

    4

    Detail is the product

    A 120 millisecond fade instead of a slow tab animation. A login that works on the first tap. The pencil taught me that people feel what they cannot name.
